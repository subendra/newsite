AI checks bottle labels locally and uses cloud support only when needed.

1. The Problems
Missing information: Missing or misaligned labels can hide product identity, traceability and safety information.
Human limitations: Repetitive manual inspection can vary with fatigue, reduced attention and inconsistent judgement.
Rigid automation: Rule-based vision can become unreliable when lighting, bottle position or background changes.
2. How the System Works

Fast, private local inspection

YOLO11n runs in NCNN format on Raspberry Pi 4, keeping image processing close to the camera and reducing cloud dependence.

3. Why It Matters
  • Commercial: Supports more consistent automated quality inspection and reduces dependence on continuous manual checking.
  • Social & Safety: Helps reduce the risk of missing or misaligned label reaching customers.
  • Environmental & Operational: Selective offloading avoids sending every images to the cloud, reducing unnecessary data transmission and cloud-processing activity.
